# 04. Step-by-Step Alternator Removal Procedures

## Engine Bay Constraints
The first-generation Honda Fit (GD1 / 1.3L L13A) engine bay is exceptionally compact. The alternator sits below the intake runners and behind the radiator core support, with minimal radial clearance. Two distinct extraction pathways exist:

---

## Tooling Checklist

- **10mm Socket / Wrench:** Negative battery cable and B+ output terminal nut.
- **12mm Deep & Shallow Sockets:** Top slider bolts, lower pivot bolt, EGR nuts.
- **14mm Long Wrench / Breaker Bar:** Belt tensioner relief / pivot bolt.
- **6-inch 3/8" Drive Extension:** Essential for lower pivot bolt access near oil filter.
- **Pry Bar / Flathead Screwdriver:** For tensioning belt and wiggling alternator off brackets.
- **Trim Clip Pliers:** For plastic bumper clips (if using Method A).

---

## Method A: The Front Bumper Approach (Recommended)

*Preferred by professional technicians because it avoids disturbing intake vacuum connections and manifold studs.*

```text
[Step 1] Disconnect Battery
         Disconnect negative (-) terminal using 10mm wrench. Prevent fire/arc risk.

[Step 2] Remove Front Bumper Cover
         Remove plastic push clips along top radiator grille and lower splash shield.
         Disconnect front fog lights (if equipped) and pull bumper cover off.

[Step 3] Swing Radiator Forward
         Unbolt upper radiator and A/C condenser stay brackets (10mm).
         Gently tilt radiator assembly 2–3 inches forward toward front of vehicle.
         (DO NOT disconnect coolant hoses or A/C refrigerant lines).

[Step 4] Remove Belt & Hardware
         Relieve belt tension and slip off alternator pulley.
         Unplug 4-pin green connector and remove 10mm B+ output nut.
         Unbolt upper 12mm adjustment bolts.
         Reach underneath car (above oil filter) with 12mm socket + 6" extension to pull long pivot bolt.

[Step 5] Straight-Line Extraction
         Slide alternator forward through the opened radiator window.
```

---

## Method B: The Top-Engine Approach (Bumper Remains On)

*Useful if working without axle stands or if bumper clips are brittle/damaged.*

1. **Intake Plumbing:** Unbolt air filter housing and remove intake ducting.
2. **EGR Valve Removal:** Unbolt the 12mm nuts securing the EGR valve.
3. **CRITICAL STEP — Stud Extraction:** You **must** remove the threaded EGR mounting studs out of the cylinder head using the "double nut" trick or an external Torx / stud puller. If the studs remain in the block, they physically block the alternator from sliding up and out.
4. **Harness Clearance:** Unbolt wiring harness bracket from cylinder head to create 2 inches of harness slack.
5. **Wiggle Outward:** Once mounting bolts are extracted, rotate the alternator pulley upwards and twist it through the space cleared by the removed EGR valve.

---

## Torque & Reinstallation Specifications

- **Bottom Long Pivot Bolt (12mm/14mm):** `45 N·m (33 lb-ft)`
- **Upper Slider / Mounting Bolts (12mm):** `22 N·m (16 lb-ft)`
- **B+ Battery Cable Output Nut (10mm):** `8 N·m (6 lb-ft)` *(Do not over-torque; internal insulator is brittle plastic)*
- **Belt Tension:** 5–10 mm deflection under thumb pressure on longest belt span.
