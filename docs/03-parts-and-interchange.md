## 2. Technical Specifications

- **Nominal Voltage:** 12V / 14V
- **Rated Output:** 80 Amperes
- **Pulley Configuration:** 5-Groove serpentine pulley
- **Rotation:** Clockwise (viewed from pulley end)
- **Mounting:** 2 upper bracket ears (12mm bolts), 1 lower long pivot ear (12mm/14mm bolt)
- **Harness Plug:** 4-pin oval green/gray connector (`IG`, `C`, `FR`, `L`)

### Rebuild Feasibility Note
- The internal regulator (`31150-PWA-004`) is **hard-soldered** to the rectifier bridge plate.
- Standard DIY mechanics without desoldering stations cannot replace just the regulator. An electrical rebuild shop or complete swapped unit is recommended.
