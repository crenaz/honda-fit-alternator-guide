## 2. Emergency "No-Tow" Short Drive Strategy

Because the vehicle is within 1.6 km of the primary workshop area, you can avoid a CI$75+ flatbed tow:

1. **Staging Jump:** Connect helper vehicle jumper cables for 10–15 minutes to infuse charge into the depleted battery.
2. **Absolute Minimal Load Rule:**
   - **A/C & Cabin Fan:** Dial to **0** (DO NOT leave at position 4; blower motor pulls >20A).
   - **Headlights:** OFF.
   - **Stereo / Accessories:** OFF.
3. **Transit Window:** With only the fuel pump and ignition coils energized, a 12V automotive battery can sustain the engine for 5 to 10 minutes of driving. Drive immediately to the repair shop.
